# Privacy Policy for Intervexa

**Last updated:** October 2026

Intervexa ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the **Intervexa** mobile application for iOS.

## 1. Information We Process

### Voice and Speech Data
- Intervexa offers voice-based mock interview practice. 
- When you use voice mode, your speech is captured via your device's microphone and transcribed to text using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework.
- We do not store, sell, or share raw audio recordings with third-party advertisers.

### Profile & Practice Data
- Practice interview responses, scores, focus areas, and job preferences (e.g., target role, experience level) are stored locally on your device using Apple SwiftData.
- You have full control over your practice history and can delete past interview sessions or reset your data at any time from within the App Settings.

## 2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions
All financial transactions, subscriptions, and consumable pack purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Intervexa does not collect or store your credit card details or payment credentials.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Intervexa does not sell your personal data to third parties or data brokers.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Intervexa is intended for job seekers and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
